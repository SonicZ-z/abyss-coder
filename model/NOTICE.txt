Abyss Coder 0.5B — branding and model attribution

Abyss Coder is the local assistant name and branding created for @fakesonicz_ on Discord. The file is not named after the Discord handle.

The GGUF distributed here is a renamed and quantized build based on Qwen2.5-Coder-0.5B-Instruct. The underlying model weights were created by the Qwen team; the Abyss branding does not claim those weights were trained from scratch by @fakesonicz_ or Abyss.
